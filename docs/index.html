What this covers, and its limits

Address lookup uses Forsyth County's own public geocoder — no third-party service involved.

Nearby parcels come live from the county's parcel GIS layer, rebuilt nightly from tax/assessment records.

The red "possible candidate" flag means a PUD-zoned parcel currently shows no assessed building area — a reasonable proxy for undeveloped land, but assessed value lags real construction by a full cycle. A parcel can be under active construction and still show flagged here.

Alerts cover new Rezoning, Zoning Condition Amendment, and Site Development filings countywide — checked once daily against the county's permit portal, not in real time.
